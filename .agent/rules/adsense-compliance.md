# Google AdSense Compliance Rules

To protect the Google AdSense account from policy violations, invalid traffic strikes, and account suspensions, all code and layouts must follow these mandatory rules:

1. **No Accidental Clicks**:
   - Maintain a minimum **25px margin** between any AdSense ad slot and interactive buttons (Download, Next, Previous, Search).
   - Never place an ad unit directly underneath a "Download Wallpaper" button where a misclick could occur.

2. **No Deceptive Labels**:
   - Only use the exact labels **"Advertisements"** or **"Sponsored Links"** above ad units.
   - Do NOT use phrases like "Download Link", "Featured Partner", "Recommended Tools", or "Support Us".

3. **Content-to-Ad Ratio**:
   - Ensure primary content (wallpaper gallery or blog text) exceeds advertising screen space.
   - Never have more than 1 sticky ad visible at any time.
   - Do not display ads on empty pages, error 404 pages, or login screens.

4. **Mandatory Legal Pages**:
   - Ensure the site always links to Privacy Policy (with Google cookie clause), Terms of Service, About Us, Contact Us, and DMCA policy in the global footer.
