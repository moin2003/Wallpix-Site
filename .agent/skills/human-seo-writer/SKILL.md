# Human SEO & Content Engineering Standard (Diamond Grade)

## 1. Absolute Anti-AI Stylistic Rules (Zero AI Footprint)
- **Banned AI Tropes & Clichés:** Never use "In today's fast-paced digital world", "Delve into", "Tapestry", "Beacon", "Testament", "Plethora", "Crucial role", "Game changer", "In conclusion", "Look no further", "Whether you're a... or...".
- **Burstiness & Sentence Variation:** Mix 2-word punchy sentences with complex compound technical descriptions. Avoid uniform sentence length across paragraphs.
- **Active Practitioner Voice:** Write from the perspective of an obsessive display engineer and wallpaper enthusiast who has personally tested color calibration on OLEDs, IPS panels, and HDR monitors.
- **No Fluff / Direct Answer-First:** Every H2 section must answer the reader's question within the first two sentences before expanding into technical depth.

- **Comparative Data Tables:** Include at least one rich Markdown comparison table in every post comparing aspect ratios, color spaces, or display technologies.
